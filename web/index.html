Stroke Optimization

To keep writing lag-free, Boox tablets record far more pen data than actually needed. While this is great for responsiveness, it results in unnecessarily large files filled with redundant points—like saving hundreds of overlapping dots just to draw a simple straight line.

Instead of blindly smoothing out your lines and altering your handwriting, this app safely cleans up the extra data. It analyzes the exact position, pressure, and tilt of your strokes, and removes a point only if the change is completely invisible to the human eye.

This results in a drastically smaller file sizes with zero loss in visual quality.
