How does Edit Online work?

Your drawing is copied to the clipboard as SVG, then Boxy SVG opens in a new tab. You may need to allow clipboard permissions for the editor. Use File → New From Clipboard to paste it. You can also download the .svg and edit it in any SVG editor like Inkscape.

How to get your .note file:

  1. Open your Boox Notes app.

How it Works

Optimize .note files

Boox tablets record far more pen data than needed to keep writing smooth. This tool removes redundant points while preserving position, pressure, and tilt — your handwriting looks identical, but files shrink by 5–10x.

Import SVGs

Convert SVG drawings into .note format so you can view and annotate them on your Boox device. Shapes, paths, and text are supported.

Preview & export

See a side-by-side comparison of original vs optimized, export as PDF or SVG, and simulate how colors look on your e-ink display.

Everything runs in your browser — your files are never uploaded anywhere.
